outconv (round 7, stage C): the single host output conversion of the device mkFit final fit
=============================================================================================
Owner files: plugins/MkFitAlpakaOutputTrackConverter.cc, plugins/MkFitAlpakaOutputTrackCompare.cc, doc/outconv.txt.
Customise (proposed for python/, menu lane owns it): L/r7_outconv/patches/customizeOutConv.py.

1. WHAT RUNS TODAY (target menu, device fit from build)
  hltInitialStepTrackCandidatesMkFitFitDevice (device fit, TrackSoA) -> host copy (framework)
  -> hltInitialStepTrackCandidatesMkFitFit = MkFitAlpakaOutputWrapperFromTrackSoA (TrackSoA -> mkfit::TrackVec)
  -> hltInitialStepTracks = stock MkFitOutputTrackConverter (RecoTracker/MkFit/plugins/MkFitOutputTrackConverter.cc)
  The stock converter per candidate (no per-hit states in the menu):
   a) state quality cuts, CCS -> global curvilinear, FreeTrajectoryState, Sylvester positive-definiteness checks;
   b) on-track hits: clone of every legacy rechit into an OwnVector (OT: a new Phase2TrackerRecHit1D with the yy error
      at float max); the OwnVector is then COPIED (clones again) into hitsVecs, and the output collection is filled by
      a THIRD clone; ordering = OwnVector::sort (std::sort) with a comparator that recomputes det()->subDetector(),
      globalPosition() and the TOB side on every comparison;
   c) the PCA: TSOS on the first hit's surface, TSCBLBuilderNoMaterial to the beam line with the CMSSW MagneticField;
   d) hit pattern: appendHits;
   e) missing inner / outer hits: NavigationSchool::compatibleLayers(innermost / outermost layer, fts at the first hit)
      and for every compatible layer compatibleDets(first-hit TSOS, PropagatorWithMaterial[Opposite], Chi2 estimator):
      the front det -> missing_inner/outer or inactive_inner/outer from MeasurementTrackerEvent;
   f) TrackExtra (seed ref, zero traj params), SeedStopInfo (unfilled).
  Consumers of the missing inner/outer pattern: the HP Torch classifier features lostInnerHits / lostOuterHits
  (TrackFeatureExtractor), so (e) is physics for the HP selection.

2. THE NEW MODULE: MkFitAlpakaOutputTrackConverter (host, global)
  Input: the device fit's TrackSoA (host copy) + its status product; legacy hits through the index-only
  MkFitClusterIndexToHit maps; seeds; MeasurementTrackerEvent; beam spot; ES as the stock converter. No
  MkFitOutputWrapper, no MkFitEventOfHits (is_pixel per layer from MkFitGeometry's TrackerInfo, which is what
  LayerOfHits::is_pixel returns), no MkFitSeedWrapper (unused by the stock code).
  Same operations as the stock converter in the same order (a, c, d, e, f unchanged CMSSW calls), except:
   - hits are referenced while the track is built and cloned ONCE into the output (stock: three clones);
   - ordering keys (position, barrel flag, tilted-TOB flag) computed once per hit; the stock comparator is applied by
     a stable insertion (no general sort). std::sort (stock) is not stable, and the comparator is not a strict weak
     order for mixed mag2/perp2 pairs, so ~4e-4 of the tracks list two such hits in the other order (TrackExtra only;
     see SIMPLIFICATIONS). validateStockOrder = True uses std::sort: then every field is identical to stock.
     29% of the tracks have > 16 on-track hits (Phase-2 PS/2S double hits), counted in the timing summary.
  The comparator only asks isBarrel(subDetector()): the flag comes from the DetId subdetector (PXB/TIB/TOB), checked
  per hit against det()->subDetector() when validateStockOrder is on (identical on 133521 tracks).
  Refused: TrajectoryInEvent (mtd_at_hlt needs per-hit states the device fit does not export), Phase-1 geometry.
  Instrumentation (untracked, off in the menu): timing (per-section timers), fieldStudy (the field question, sec. 4,
  and the missing-hit bookkeeping study, sec. 5).
  Acceptance: MkFitAlpakaOutputTrackCompare, field by field (params, covariance, chi2/ndof/charge/algo, the three
  hit-pattern categories word by word, seed ref, every rechit's detId/type/cluster/local position and error) against
  the stock converter in the same job on the same TrackSoA -> must be IDENTICAL.

4. THE FIELD QUESTION
4. THE FIELD QUESTION
  The stock converter's PCA uses the CMSSW MagneticField at the first-hit state (TSCBLBuilderNoMaterial: helix with
  the field at the start point). A device PCA would use mkFit's parabolic Bz(z, r) with #186's constants. The study
  recomputes the PCA with a MagneticField stand-in returning mkFit's Bz (no Br, as mkFit's helix) and reports
  |Bz_mkFit/Bz_CMSSW - 1| at the first-hit states and the reco::Track parameter pulls (mkFit field - CMSSW field) /
  sigma.
  RESULT (ttbar, 133510 tracks): |Bz_mkFit/Bz_CMSSW - 1| at the first-hit states mean 2.0e-4, max 5.8e-4.
  Tracks with |pull| >= 1e-2 (qoverp, lambda, phi, dxy, dsz): 0, 0, 4209 (3.2e-2), 54 (4.0e-4), 5 (3.7e-5);
  >= 1e-1 and >= 1: none. Isolated-fit reco::Track floor (D7-a, r7_fitgap floors/isolated/floor_ttbar_100_cpe1_
  fitisotrk.txt) at >= 1e-2: 1.4e-3, 4.1e-3, 9.5e-4, 9.4e-4, 5.6e-2. phi is 33x over the floor (a coherent bias:
  the ~2e-4 field difference over the first-hit -> PCA extrapolation), the rest inside.
  VERDICT: a device PCA with mkFit's field is NOT inside the floor. Keep the PCA with the CMSSW MagneticField: on the
  host (1.5 ms per tracked event, kept in this module), or on the device with Bz sampled from the menu's MagneticField
  (an (r, z) grid ES product filled at IOV time, or Bz at each first-hit module from a per-module table) - only then
  is a device PCA a rounding-level change.

5. THE MISSING-HIT PATTERN FROM MKFIT'S BOOKKEEPING (study, for the device design)
  mkFit records crossed layers without a hit as -1 (miss) / -7 (inactive) entries in the hit list. The study tables
  compare, per track, the navigation's missing inner / outer counts with the -1/-7 entries before the first / after
  the last valid entry and in total.
  RESULT (ttbar, 133510 tracks): navigation finds >= 1 missing inner hit on 14% of the tracks, mkFit has a -1/-7
  entry before the first valid hit on < 1%; >= 1 missing outer hit on 24%, mkFit tail misses on 0.4%. mkFit's
  bookkeeping is NOT a proxy for the CMSSW missing-hit pattern (building stops at the last hit; LST seeds already
  carry the outer hits). A device missing-hit pattern needs its own per-track propagation to the compatible layers.

6. NEXT STEPS (stage C on the device), ranked by host ms
  a) Device missing-hit navigation (55-60% of the module): per track, the inner/outer compatible-layer lists of the
     SimpleNavigationSchool as an ES table (per mkFit layer and direction), the device state propagated to each listed
     layer (mkFit propagation to the layer's r or z), the module hit there (EventOfHits module table) and its activity
     (per-event inactive-module mask from the MeasurementTrackerEvent). Physics: lostInnerHits / lostOuterHits are
     HP Torch features, so this is a switch-gated change (OFF) with the per-track count agreement table and the HP
     decision comparison (hpsel's in-job comparator), then MTV / paired truth per O6-1.
  b) PCA on the device with the CMSSW field (see 4): saves ~1.5 ms; output sparse (5 params + 15 cov per track).
  c) Hit ordering keys on the device from the HitSoA positions + a module (subdet, tilt) table: the host only reads an
     index order (~3-4 ms of 'hits + order'); the clones stay until stage D slims the legacy hit collections.
