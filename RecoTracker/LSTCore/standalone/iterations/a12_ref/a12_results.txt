L1. THE BAND. `C_aX5` (-a 5 -XCT 5) came out BIT-IDENTICAL to `B_a5X1` (-a 5 -XC 1, the
    bare-chain arm switched OFF): all 14 headline metrics to 5 decimals, all 12 per-region

    structural, and it reframes the round's tuned constant:

      the pre-existing -RPS predicate (main.cc:3442) already retires EVERY seed whose best
      chain logit is >= -a, unconditionally. The ported CrossCleanpLS bare-chain arm
      retires a seed whose best chain logit is >= -XCT *and* which sits within dR^2 < 0.02
      of a bare chain TC. So the arm is a strict subset of -RPS above -a, and the ONLY
      band in which it can ever act is  [ -XCT , -a ).

    In one sentence, the assembled algorithm's seed policy is:
      logit >= -a           MERGE the seed into the chain (one longer track)
      -XCT <= logit < -a    DELETE the seed (two rows -> one row)
      logit <  -XCT         KEEP the seed as a bare type-8 row
    The round tuned the DELETE band (-XCT, with -a pinned at its stale 6.875). This angle
    moves the MERGE/DELETE boundary instead, and merging strictly dominates deleting: it
    keeps the efficiency, removes the duplicate, and makes the track LONGER.
